macOS Finder

Mount on Your MacBook

  1. Open Finder and press ⌘ Cmd + K.
  2. Paste your Drive Link above into the server address field.
  3. Click Connect and select Guest (or enter your Phone & PIN).
  4. Your card mounts under Locations just like an external SSD.
Windows 10 / 11

Map as Drive Z:\

  1. Open File Explorer and select This PC.
  2. Click Map Network Drive in the top toolbar.
  3. Choose Z: and paste your Drive Link into the Folder box.
  4. Click Finish to access your files directly from any app.
